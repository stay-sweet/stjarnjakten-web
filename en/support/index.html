Support

A little help for your family’s everyday adventures.

Contact the developer
Parents and guardians can email Stay Sweet at info@staysweet.dev for help, feedback or feature requests.

For a technical issue, include your app version, the iOS, iPadOS or Android version, device model, and a short description of what happened and what you expected. Please leave out children's names, parent PINs and other private family information, including in screenshots.

Star Hunt is in closed beta and is not yet in the App Store or on Google Play. The answers below apply to iPhone, iPad and Android unless a heading says otherwise.

Forgotten your parent PIN?

iPhone and iPad: open Parent mode and choose “Forgot the code?”. Follow the device authentication prompts to create a new PIN. Recovery uses Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode.

Android: open Parent mode and choose “Forgot your PIN?”. Reset it with the device’s screen lock (PIN, password, pattern or biometrics).

On both platforms, anyone who knows the device’s passcode or screen lock can recover parent access.

Restore a Family purchase

One profile is free; Family is a one-time purchase for additional profiles. Some closed-beta versions include Family without a purchase.

iPhone and iPad: on the Family purchase screen, choose “Restore purchase” and use the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Android: purchases belong to your Google Play account. On the Family purchase screen, choose the restore option and use the Google Play account that made the purchase. Restoring brings back Family access, not deleted profiles.

Where is my family's data?

By default, profiles, missions, stars, goals and history are stored on your device. No account is needed and the developer cannot retrieve these records. On iPhone and iPad, ordinary iCloud or computer backups may include app data. On Android, device backup and device transfer may include the app’s local data, but not the parent PIN, which stays on the device.

Share a family with other adults (optional)

Family sharing is optional and currently available only in development test builds. When you connect a family, its profiles, missions, stars, goals and history are kept on the developer’s server so adults and children’s devices can stay in step. The person who created the family signs in with an email code; other adults and children’s devices join with a code. Read the privacy policy for what is stored.

  • Leave a family as an adult: choose “Leave the family”. Your devices lose access; the children’s profiles and progress stay with the family.
  • Disconnect one device: choose “Disconnect this device”. The shared copy is removed from that device and your original local family is shown again.
  • Delete the shared family: the person who created it can choose “Delete shared family”. See Delete account and data.

Mission reminders

Optional reminders are scheduled by your device and work without an account or internet connection. If you do not receive them, check that notifications are allowed for Star Hunt in your device settings.

Export my data

For a connected family, adults can use “Export My Data” in Settings to save a readable file of the family’s information. Data that is only on your device is not on a server, so there is nothing to export from the developer; ask us if you need help.

Delete local data

In Parent mode, open Settings and choose the option to delete all data, then confirm. This removes the local family data and parent PIN. It does not remove device backups or your Apple or Google Play purchase entitlement. To delete a shared family or an account, see Delete account and data.
