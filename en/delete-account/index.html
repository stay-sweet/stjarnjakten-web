Delete account and data

How to delete your family’s data, leave a shared family or delete your account.

Most families have nothing to delete online. Star Hunt works without an account and keeps profiles, missions, stars, goals and history on your device. An online account exists only if you choose to share a family with other adults and devices.

Delete data on this device

In Parent mode, open Settings and choose the option to delete all data (on Android: “Delete all data and show the welcome guide”), then confirm. This permanently removes every profile, mission, star, goal, history entry and the parent PIN from the device. You can also uninstall the app. Device backups, and purchases with Apple or Google Play, are managed by those services and are not removed.

Delete a shared family or your account

If you connected a family, the person who created it can end it from the app, using device verification:

  • Delete shared family: deletes the online family for everyone. Other devices lose access immediately.
  • Delete account: if you created the family, this also deletes the shared family for everyone. It deletes your sign-in details, including your email address. The original local family on your device is kept.
  • Delete child profile: deletes one child’s shared profile and disconnects that child’s devices.

Deletion cannot be undone. Use “Export My Data” first if you want a copy of the shared family’s information.

Leave a shared family

  • An adult who joined: choose “Leave the family”. Your devices lose access. The children’s profiles and progress stay with the family. Choosing “Delete account” does the same and also deletes your account.
  • One device: choose “Disconnect this device”. The shared copy and unsent requests are removed from that device, the online family stays, and your original local family is shown again.

What happens on the server, and when

  • Immediately: when a family is deleted, access is revoked for every device and its profiles, missions, stars, goals, history, members and invitations are deleted. When an account is deleted, its sign-in is removed so it can no longer be used. Push notification registrations for removed devices are deleted.
  • In the background, usually within minutes: the server removes the remaining technical records, such as sync receipts and leftover sign-in sessions, in small batches, then the family record itself.
  • Backups: the hosting provider, Convex, may keep infrastructure backups for a limited time under its own retention rules. They are not used to restore a deleted family.
  • Not affected: data on devices (delete it as described above), device backups, Apple and Google Play purchase records, and support emails you have sent us.

Ask without the app

If you cannot open the app, email info@staysweet.dev from the address you used to sign in and ask us to delete your account and shared family. We may ask you to confirm in a way that proves the address is yours. We aim to answer within a week and complete deletion within 30 days. To delete support emails or beta feedback we hold, ask in the same message.
