Privacy policy

How we handle information, in plain language.

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This policy explains how Stjärnjakten, called Star Hunt in English, handles information in its iPhone and iPad app, its TestFlight beta, and this website.

The app is provided by Stay Sweet in Sweden. The developer is responsible for personal information received through support and beta testing. Contact: info@staysweet.dev.

Your family's everyday information stays on your device. No account is required. The app does not upload child profiles, missions, stars, goals, or history to the developer or the catalog service. Apple handles TestFlight diagnostics separately, as explained below.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores profile names, ages and chosen icons; mission text, schedules and completion status; stars; selected goals; completion and redemption history; and app preferences. This information is used to provide the app's family routines and progress features. Age is used on the device to select appropriate suggestions.

There is no hosted family account or live synchronization between devices. App data may be included in ordinary iCloud or computer device backups according to your backup settings. The developer cannot access those backups.

The app is designed for a shared device. People using child mode can switch between available child profiles. Parent mode protects planning, approvals, profile management and deletion; it does not make each child's profile private from other people using child mode.

2. Parent PIN and device authentication

The parent PIN and the app's biometric access setting are stored in Apple's Keychain with protection tied to this device. They are not sent to the developer or transferred to another device through Keychain synchronization.

If you enable Face ID or Touch ID, iOS performs authentication. The app receives the result and never receives your face or fingerprint data. Forgotten-PIN recovery uses iOS device authentication, which may include the device passcode. A trusted device owner can therefore recover parent access.

3. Public suggestions and network connections

The app downloads a public catalog of mission ideas, goal ideas and icons from Convex. The request contains no family content, child's age, profile identifier, or app-generated device or installation identifier. Suggestions are filtered on the device. The catalog is also bundled and cached so everyday use can continue offline.

Making an internet connection necessarily exposes the connection's IP address and technical request information to the hosting infrastructure. Convex and its infrastructure providers may process this information to deliver and secure the service. It is not used by the app to build family profiles or track children. See Convex's privacy information.

4. Purchases and Apple services

The app uses Apple's StoreKit service for Family product availability and purchase entitlements. If purchases are available in your version, Apple processes payment and provides transaction information so the app can unlock or restore the purchase. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. An in-app data reset does not cancel an Apple purchase or delete Apple's transaction records. See Apple's privacy information.

5. Diagnostics, TestFlight and feedback

The app has no advertising, third-party analytics or session-replay software. Its own diagnostic logging uses Apple's on-device logging system and avoids family text, PINs and persistent profile identifiers. Apple may provide app diagnostic reports through its distribution services under the applicable Apple settings and terms.

When you use TestFlight, Apple automatically shares crash and usage information with the developer, including technical device and app details. Your name and email may be visible when you join by email invitation; joining solely through a public link does not expose them to the developer. Feedback you submit can include comments and screenshots. The developer uses this information to investigate problems and improve the app, and does not share TestFlight information with third parties. See TestFlight & Privacy.

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and what you choose to send. Avoid including children's full names, PINs or other unnecessary private information in messages and screenshots. The developer and the email service used to handle the correspondence process that information to answer your request.

6. Retention and deletion

Family information remains on your device until you delete it. In Parent mode, open Profiles → Settings and choose the option to delete all data. A successful reset removes profiles, missions, stars, goals, history, parent credentials and app preferences. A public catalog cache may remain. Previous iCloud or computer backups and Apple's purchase records are managed separately and are not removed by this reset.

The developer keeps support correspondence and any separately retained beta feedback only while needed to answer the request, resolve the reported issue, or meet a legal obligation, and deletes it when those purposes no longer apply. Information retained within Apple's services follows Apple's retention rules; a local app reset does not delete TestFlight records. Contact the developer to request access to or deletion of support or beta information held by the developer.

7. Children and privacy choices

The app is intended for families with children aged 3–12 and adult supervision. Children do not need an online account, email address or real name to use it. Family records are not sold, used for advertising, or uploaded to the public catalog. Parents can choose nicknames, manage local information, turn off biometric access in Settings, and delete app data.

8. Your rights

For personal information received through support or beta testing, the developer relies on the legitimate interests of answering requests and improving the app, subject to your rights, and on legal obligations where applicable. There is no automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal information, and object to processing. Contact the developer using the email above. Family records held only on your device must be managed there because the developer has no remote access. You may also complain to Sweden's privacy authority, IMY, or your local supervisory authority.

9. This website and service providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, embedded media or cookies added by the developer. If you choose a language, the website remembers only that preference in your browser’s local storage. You can remove it by clearing this website’s data in your browser. GitHub processes technical visitor information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Apple, Convex, GitHub and email-service providers may process service information in countries outside Sweden or the EU/EEA. Their privacy information describes their handling, retention and transfer safeguards. Family records are not sent to Convex or GitHub by the app.

10. Changes and contact

This page will be updated when the app's data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to info@staysweet.dev.
