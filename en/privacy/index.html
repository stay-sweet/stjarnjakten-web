Privacy policy

How we handle information, in plain language.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how Stjärnjakten, called Star Hunt in English, handles information in its iPhone, iPad and Android apps, their beta testing, and this website. The apps are not yet in the App Store or on Google Play.

The app is provided by Stay Sweet in Sweden. The developer is responsible for personal information received through support, beta testing and optional family sharing. Contact: info@staysweet.dev.

Your family's everyday information stays on your device. No account is required. By default the app does not upload child profiles, missions, stars, goals, or history to the developer. Only if you choose to share a family with other adults and devices is that family's information kept on the developer's server, as explained in section 3. There is no advertising and no tracking.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores profile names, ages and chosen icons; mission text, schedules and completion status; stars; selected goals; completion and redemption history; and app preferences. This information is used to provide the app's family routines and progress features. Age is used on the device to select appropriate suggestions.

On iPhone and iPad, app data may be included in ordinary iCloud or computer backups according to your backup settings. On Android, device backup and device transfer may include the app's local database. The developer cannot access those backups. The parent PIN is not included in backups.

The app is designed for a shared device. People using child mode can switch between available child profiles. Parent mode protects planning, approvals, profile management and deletion; it does not make each child's profile private from other people using child mode.

2. Parent PIN and device authentication

On iPhone and iPad, the parent PIN and the biometric access setting are stored in Apple's Keychain with protection tied to this device. On Android, the PIN is stored only as a salted verifier encrypted with the Android Keystore, together with failed-attempt counts, in storage that is excluded from backups. They are not sent to the developer or transferred to another device.

If you enable Face ID, Touch ID or Android biometrics, the operating system performs authentication. The app receives the result and never receives your face or fingerprint data. Forgotten-PIN recovery uses the device's authentication (the iOS passcode or the Android screen lock). A trusted device owner can therefore recover parent access.

3. Optional family sharing

Family sharing lets adults and children's devices work on the same family. It is off by default, needs an internet connection, and is currently available only in development test builds. Your local family stays on the device and is not uploaded unless you choose to connect.

If you connect a family, the following is kept on the developer's server, hosted by Convex:

  • Email address of the person who creates the family. It is used to sign in with a one-time code. The code email is sent through the email provider Resend. Other adults and children's devices do not need an email address; they join with a one-time code and are given anonymous identities.
  • Display names you choose for profiles and adults (nicknames are recommended), and the family's profiles, missions, schedules, stars, goals, completion history, and which devices belong to the family.
  • Join requests. A join code has 8 characters, works once and expires after 10 minutes. Anyone who has the code sees the family name and the owner's display name before asking to join. A request carries the name the person chooses, and only the family's owner can approve it. Declining a request ends the code.
  • Push notification tokens for devices that turn on notifications, sent to Apple's or Google's push services so a device can be told that something changed. Notification text carries no names or mission text.
  • Technical records needed to sync safely, such as sign-in sessions, device registrations and operation receipts.

Family information is used only to run the family and is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with others. Adults in the family can see the family's information, and anyone in a family with access to a connected device can see what that device shows. The camera may be used to scan a join code (section 5).

4. Reminders, public suggestions and notices

Local reminders. If you turn on optional mission reminders, your device schedules them itself. The reminder time and text stay on the device; nothing is sent to a server, and no account is needed.

Suggestions and notices. The app downloads a public catalog of mission ideas, goal ideas and icons, and public notices from the developer (for example, news about the app), from Convex. These requests are anonymous. They contain no family content, child's age, profile identifier, or app-generated device or installation identifier, and the app does not track which notices you read. Suggestions and notices are filtered or shown on the device. The catalog is also bundled and cached so everyday use can continue offline.

Making an internet connection necessarily exposes the connection's IP address and technical request information to the hosting infrastructure. Convex and its infrastructure providers may process this information to deliver and secure the service. It is not used by the app to build family profiles or track children. See Convex's privacy information.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications are requested only when you turn on reminders or family notifications. The app works without them.
  • Camera is requested only when you scan a family join code. You can type the code instead. Images are not stored or sent.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or biometrics are optional and used only to open parent mode.

6. Export My Data

For a connected family, adults can use “Export My Data” in Settings to receive one readable file with the family's information held on the server. The file is created on request, sent only to the device that asked, and saved or shared where you choose. Information that is only on your device is already yours and is not part of the export.

7. Purchases and store services

The app uses Apple's StoreKit on iPhone and iPad and Google Play Billing on Android for Family product availability and purchase entitlements. If purchases are available in your version, Apple or Google processes payment and provides transaction information so the app can unlock or restore the purchase. The developer does not receive your payment details, and purchase information is not sent to the developer's server. An in-app data reset does not cancel a purchase or delete the store's records. See Apple's privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

8. Diagnostics, beta testing and feedback

The app has no advertising, third-party analytics, crash-reporting or session-replay software. Its own diagnostic logging uses the system's on-device logging and avoids family text, PINs and persistent profile identifiers. Apple and Google may provide app diagnostic reports through their distribution services under their applicable settings and terms.

When you use TestFlight, Apple automatically shares crash and usage information with the developer, including technical device and app details. Your name and email may be visible when you join by email invitation; joining solely through a public link does not expose them to the developer. Testers on Google Play are handled under Google's testing terms. Feedback you submit can include comments and screenshots. The developer uses this information to investigate problems and improve the app, and does not share beta information with third parties. See TestFlight & Privacy.

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and what you choose to send. Avoid including children's full names, PINs or other unnecessary private information in messages and screenshots. The developer and the email service used to handle the correspondence process that information to answer your request.

9. Retention and deletion

Family information remains on your device until you delete it. In Parent mode, open Settings and choose the option to delete all data. A successful reset removes profiles, missions, stars, goals, history, the parent PIN and app preferences. A public catalog cache may remain. Device backups and store purchase records are managed separately and are not removed by this reset.

A connected family remains on the server until the person who created it deletes it or deletes their account. Deleting the family revokes access for every device immediately and deletes its profiles, history, members and invitations; the remaining technical records are then purged in the background, usually within minutes. Deleting an account removes its sign-in immediately. Anonymous identities that never joined a family, or no longer belong to one, are deleted after 30 days. When someone is removed from a family, a note that their device was removed is kept for 30 days so the device can show why it was disconnected. Join requests are deleted shortly after they are answered or expire. Adults can leave a family, and any device can disconnect itself. The steps are on the Delete account and data page, which also explains how to ask us to delete data without the app. The hosting provider may keep infrastructure backups for a limited time under its own rules.

The developer keeps support correspondence and any separately retained beta feedback only while needed to answer the request, resolve the reported issue, or meet a legal obligation, and deletes it when those purposes no longer apply. Information retained within Apple's or Google's services follows their retention rules. Contact the developer to request access to or deletion of support or beta information held by the developer.

10. Children and privacy choices

The app is intended for families with children aged 3–12 and adult supervision. It is set up and managed by an adult. Children do not need an online account, email address or real name to use it, and the app contains no advertising. Purchases and outside links are behind the parent area. Only if an adult connects a family does the family's information, including children's chosen display names, progress and device registrations, go to the server, and only for running that family. Family records are not sold, used for advertising, or uploaded to the public catalog. Parents can choose nicknames, manage information, turn off biometric access, export, and delete data.

11. Your rights

For personal information received through support, beta testing or family sharing, the developer relies on the performance of the service you asked for, the legitimate interests of answering requests and improving the app, subject to your rights, and on legal obligations where applicable. There is no automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal information, and object to processing. Use the in-app export and deletion options, or contact the developer using the email above. Family records held only on your device must be managed there because the developer has no remote access. You may also complain to Sweden's privacy authority, IMY, or your local supervisory authority.

12. This website and service providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, embedded media or cookies added by the developer. If you choose a language, the website remembers only that preference in your browser’s local storage. You can remove it by clearing this website’s data in your browser. GitHub processes technical visitor information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Apple, Google, Convex, Resend, GitHub and email-service providers may process service information in countries outside Sweden or the EU/EEA. Their privacy information describes their handling, retention and transfer safeguards. Family records are not sent to GitHub, and are sent to Convex only when you connect a family.

13. Changes and contact

This page will be updated when the app's data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to info@staysweet.dev.
